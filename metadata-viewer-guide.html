Location, Time & Device — Verified Instantly

One of the most powerful — and often sensitive — parts of image metadata is geolocation. Toolivo decodes GPS coordinates into human-readable latitude/longitude *and* displays them in a dedicated map-ready format, while clearly indicating when location data is present (or absent).

Paired with timestamps (capture time, digitization time, modification time) and device fingerprints (camera make/model, lens, firmware), you gain a complete audit trail — essential for legal, journalistic, or archival workflows.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are my files uploaded or stored anywhere?

No. Toolivo is a fully client-side image metadata viewer — all processing happens in your browser. Your images and videos are never uploaded, stored, or transmitted. Your privacy is preserved by design.

What kind of metadata can I view?

For images: full EXIF, GPS (latitude/longitude/altitude), device info (camera, lens, firmware), timestamps, and technical details (aperture, shutter speed, ISO, focal length). For videos: duration, resolution, creation/modification dates, and basic format info — all via our online video metadata viewer.

Which file formats are supported?

Images: JPEG, PNG, WebP, and GIF — ideal for using as an EXIF data viewer or to check image EXIF online. Videos: MP4, MOV, AVI (basic metadata only). All files are processed locally as a true free metadata viewer.

Can I extract GPS location from photos?

Yes — if geotagging is enabled on your device, Toolivo will display precise GPS coordinates (in decimal degrees), altitude, and direction. This makes it a powerful image EXIF reader online for verification or archival purposes.

Is video metadata fully supported like EXIF?

Basic metadata (duration, resolution, timestamps) is extracted directly from the file using the browser's native APIs. Advanced video metadata (e.g., codec, bitrate, GPS) requires external tools like MediaInfo — but Toolivo gives you instant, private insights without installation.

Can I copy or export metadata?

Yes. Click the copy icon next to any field to copy it to your clipboard — or use the full "Raw Data" tab to export everything as formatted JSON. Ideal for documentation, reporting, or digital forensics workflows.

Is this metadata viewer really free?

Yes. Toolivo is a completely free metadata extractor online — no sign-up, no watermarks, no premium tiers. It will remain free, open, and ad-free forever.

Why can't I see GPS or EXIF data in some images?

Some apps (e.g., messaging platforms, social media) strip EXIF on upload. Others disable geotagging by default. Try viewing the original file from your camera or phone — Toolivo will show all available data when present.

Does it work on mobile devices?

Yes — the interface is fully responsive. Snap a photo, open Toolivo on your phone, and instantly view image metadata free — no app needed.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Toolivo requires no email, login, or permissions. Just open, upload (or drag & drop), and explore — your session stays private and ephemeral.
